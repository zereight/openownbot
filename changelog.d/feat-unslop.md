### Changed

- The interface is calmer. Servers without a logo show their initials on a plain tile instead of a colour blob. The hosted server offer, the recommended plan and the connect steps no longer use gradients, glows or a moving border. Section names keep the case you wrote. Headings are no heavier than semibold.
- A new direct chat shows the agent's face, its name and "This is the start of your chat." instead of a hand-written slogan.
- The idle Dynamic Island shows a quiet dot instead of rotating emoji.
- Some sample and onboarding text no longer ends with "!".
