### Changed

- A channel's header shows `#`, the channel name and its topic, as on a chat server.
- The agent panel beside a channel is now "Now": one card for each agent with what it does. An agent that split its work shows the parts as a checklist (done, in progress, not started, failed).
- When an agent gives a part of the work to another agent, a chip under its message shows the part, its number of replies and the time of the newest reply. Select the chip to go to the newest reply.

### Fixed

- The `BOT` tag has enough contrast in the light theme.
