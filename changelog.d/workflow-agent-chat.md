### Added

- Ask an agent about a workflow. The Workflows page has a chat at the bottom right of the canvas. Pick an
  agent, ask what the workflow does, or tell the agent what to change. The agent edits the workflow
  with its own tools, and you see the change on the canvas. The chat is off for a workflow that you
  cannot change, and on a joined server.

### Changed

- When another device or an agent saves the workflow that you have open and you have no unsaved
  edits, the canvas keeps its position and your selection. Before, the canvas moved to fit the whole
  workflow again.
